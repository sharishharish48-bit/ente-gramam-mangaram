About Our Village

A place to share the stories and spirit of Mangaram.

Welcome to ENTe GRAMAM MANGARAM

Mangaram is a ward within Pandalam Municipality in Kerala’s Pathanamthitta district, set amid the lush greenery that defines the state’s landscape.

Coconut palms, rubber plantations and rolling paddy fields surround the locality, while the nearby Achankovil River adds a gentle, flowing presence to the scenery. During the monsoon, the countryside turns a deep, vivid green.

Quiet village lanes shaded by tall trees give Mangaram a peaceful, unhurried charm. This website celebrates our place, its people, local products and shared memories.

“A village is not just a place on the map — it is the people, memories and traditions we share.”
